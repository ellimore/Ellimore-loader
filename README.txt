Ellimore loader backend

Upload these files to the Railway service that hosts your Ellimore backend.

Endpoint:
  /loader.lua?key=YOUR_LICENSE_KEY

Important:
- This endpoint is intended for scripts/software you own or are authorized to distribute.
- Keep secrets in Railway Variables, never in GitHub.
- Replace protected-script.lua with your own authorized script.
- This starter checks the same licenses.json structure used by the bot.
